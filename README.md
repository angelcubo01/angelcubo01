### About me

Passionate **Salesforce Developer** based in **Salamanca, Spain**, building scalable CRM solutions on the Salesforce platform at **VIEWNEXT**. I work across the full delivery lifecycle — from Apex and Lightning Web Components to Copado-based DevOps — with a strong focus on quality, clean architecture, and measurable impact for business users.

Computer Science graduate from the **University of Salamanca (USAL)**. Continuous learner in the Salesforce ecosystem, with growing expertise in **Agentforce** and AI-assisted development workflows.

- Currently delivering Salesforce solutions with **Apex**, **LWC**, and **Copado**
- Background as **Salesforce Administrator** before moving into development
- Strong focus on **unit & regression testing**, data quality, and reliable deployments
- Always exploring how **AI** improves Salesforce delivery (Agentforce, Copado AI)
